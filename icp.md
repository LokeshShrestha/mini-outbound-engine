# ScaleBuild AI ideal customer profile

ScaleBuild AI helps businesses without a full in-house product or growth team ship software and improve acquisition. It provides web and mobile apps, SaaS builds, UI/UX, CRM integrations, AI and automation, paid ads, SEO, and email campaigns.

## Strong fit: 80-100

- Seed or Series A B2B SaaS, marketplace, or app company with a roadmap and 0-2 engineers
- Service business such as an agency, clinic, logistics company, real estate business, education company, or local franchise using spreadsheets or lacking a CRM
- Actively hiring a first developer, product manager, or marketing lead
- Running paid ads or email without automation, reporting, or attribution
- Publicly launching soon, coming soon, or visibly operating with an outdated product site

## Possible fit: 40-70

- Product company with a stale, slow, or off-brand site
- Solo founder with a post-launch product and limited growth motion
- Internal marketing without engineering, or engineering without growth support

## Poor fit: 0-39

- In-house engineering team of five or more or a mature internal platform team
- Enterprise requiring lengthy procurement or security review
- Agency reselling the same services
- Student, nonprofit, hobby, or no-budget project
- Gambling, speculative crypto, adult, or other high legal-risk vertical

Score based only on evidence present in the supplied company text. Never invent hiring, funding, team size, technology, or urgency signals.
